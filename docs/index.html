Omni Gacha


Welcome to the Omni Gacha!

You, who have stared into the infinite multiverse and been rewarded for it, now have acess to the infinite multiverse in the form of a gacha system.

Become all powerful, rebuild a dead world, kill the world, be the greatest hero, the worst villain, or anywhere in between. The world is your playground now. The power is up to luck but yours to command. The possibilities are endless, and fate is yours to make the best of.

To learn more head to the about section in the top right, or just go to the items tab and start rolling.

Stats

"The Omnigacha uses 8 stats to determine the ranking of an item, under the ""8M"" system: Magic, Memetic, Might, Mind, Motion, Moxie, Mutation, and Myth. Each category is defined below:"

Magic: The capacity to exert influence with mysterious or supernatural forces Memetic: The capacity to exert influence using ideas, images, or sounds Might: The capacity to exert influence through physical strength and durability Mind: The capacity to exert influence through mental flexibility and intelligence Motion: The capacity to exert influence through speed, agility, or dexterity Moxie: The capacity to exert influence through charisma or force of personality Mutation: The capacity to exert influence through genetic deviation from the norm Myth: The capacity to exert influence through historical or cultural significance

Ticket Levels

"Tickets share much of the same spread as the normal Chaos Gacha, but utilize a star system instead: 1 star through EX star, with 8 total levels. Each level of ticket grants access to 3 separate ranks of ticket. Currently, there is no way to adjust your luck due to the constraints of working in a spreadsheet. We apologize for the loss of functionality."

1 Star (F-D) Do a difficult, but humanly possible task- win a fight, get a job, hunt a wolf, etc 2 Star (E-C) Do a task almost peak human or supernatural- earn $10000 in a day, take down a gang, etc 3 Star (D-B) Do a highly difficult task- rob a guarded bank, seduce someone notable, take down a demon, etc 4 Star (C-A) Do a near humanly impossible task- escape certain death, defeat a dragon, pacify a powerful enemy, etc 5 Star (B-S) Do something extraordinary- seduce Satan's wife, save a city, stop an army, etc 6 Star (A-SS) Do something legendary- kill a legendary dragon, rip off an angel's wings, save a country/planet, etc 7 Star (S-SSS) Do something mythical- save a planet from a prophecy, seduce death itself, shake the world, etc EX Star (SS-EX) Do something impossible- save the universe, defeat every hero, make a star, destroy a galaxy, etc

Welcome to your Omni Gacha Experience!

This section should, hopefully, give you most of everything you need to know in order to start rolling, start writing, and otherwise start having fun with this spreadsheet! We won't go over the basics, as those are covered in other sections, so let's start with how you start your own adventure!

By and large, your Omni Gacha adventure should start off and be run the way YOU want to run it. We are, after all, making a framework here. We're not cops. How would you like your main character to get their Omni Gacha system?

Starting Circumstances

Option 1: Isekai via god/eldritch being/jackass wizard

Some bastard has thrown you into the world with the Omni Gacha! Oh no! You get one (1) 4 Star ticket and two (2) 3 Star tickets, you poor bastard.

Option 2: Non-wizard isekai with the Omni Gacha

You, either by truck, by luck, or by some other circumstance, have entered a new world with the Omni Gacha! You get two (2) 3 star tickets and three (3) 1 star tickets. If you're not an out of shape nerd with this option... good hustle, buddy!

Option 3: Self insert native to your chosen world

You, somehow, are yourself but as a native citizen of whatever world you chose- not a plot relevant character, but a regular person. You get three (3) 3 star tickets.

Option 4: Transmigration into an existing character

You, somehow, are now a character in the world you chose. If you chose a protagonist, you get a single 1 star ticket to start. If you picked a secondary, two (2) 2 star tickets. A tertiary character, three (3) 3 star tickets.

Option 5: A random character in your world gets the gacha

Not an isekai, not an insert, just writing any given character as if they woke up with the gacha. For a plot relevant character, you get two (2) 2 star tickets. For a side character, three (3) 3 star tickets.

Rolling Rules:

Look, let's be real- anyone writing a gacha fic already fudges their rolls to make sure the story keeps happening. Probably, anyway. Roll your stuff as you please, we literally cannot police it nor are we interested in doing so. Fudge your rolls, just pick random entries you like for synergies and pretend it's luck- who cares? Have fun, be interesting, don't let anyone tell you how to play with your own toys. If you really want to add something to the official Omni Gacha, please look for the link to our submission form on the front page! Our curse submissions are closed, though- 220 is enough!

Curses

Much like the standard chaos gacha, curses in the Omni Gacha can be rolled at the start of your journey, or when you get an applicable ticket for a curse. Or whenever you think it'd be interesting? Look, how you run your story is up to you, but each curse grants extra tickets, as explained on the curse page of this spreadsheet. That's really all there is to it- increasing severity grants more tickets at higher rank, so on and so forth. Try not to roll too many, otherwise you risk getting stupidly overpowered right off the bat and that's just boring, even with the curses.

Terms of Use for the Omni Gacha

If you run an Omni Gacha fic, you don't need to do much for credit or use, simply make reference somewhere that it is, in fact, an Omni Gacha fic. Include a link to this spreadsheet if you like, or link the accounts listed in the About page. Toss a couple bucks our way if you think this project is worth it, maybe! If you want. No pressure.

Abilities and Ability Slots

Abilities are, as labeled, any gacha ticket item that requires active effort to use. If you want to write your story that way, you can have your abilities take up "ability slots", wherein any ability in that slot is what you can use. You start with one, and work your way up to more slots every five abilities your roll. If you want to do that. Otherwise, go nuts. We're not cops. Run your story how you like. We're also not doing a classification system. That's beyond the scope of this project.

Skills and their Levels

We are, roughly, using skills as defined by the chaos gacha, but while many of our listed skills do not have levels, some of them do. The levels are as such:

Beginner

 You have a passing knowledge and maybe a year of on and off training with the subject at hand.

Apprentice

You have a solid platform of roughly five years of basic training with the subject at hand.

Journeyman

You have a very good platform of maybe ten years of dedicated training with the subject at hand.

Expert

You are, as stated, an expert in the field at hand, roughly 25 years of dedicated and practical experience all at once.

Master

You're an absolute master of the field at hand- roughly 100 years of skill are implanted within you all at once.

Grandmaster

You are a master of masters, a prodigy to the point that none can match you in your chosen field

Divine

You invented this field. You perfected it. You did it so well everyone else's efforts are cheap imitations.
